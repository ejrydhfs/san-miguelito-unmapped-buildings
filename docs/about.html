Unmapped buildings — San Miguelito

method, data, licences
MapMethod & data

What this is

Building footprints detected by machine learning on imagery from 2025-08-08/11 (Esri World Imagery, 30 cm) that have no counterpart in OpenStreetMap, for the district of San Miguelito, Panama City (49.9 km², Panama City). 62,709 candidates over ~11.3 km²; OSM currently maps 12,088 of the 71,060 buildings detected in the district (17%).

Pipeline

  1. Boundary & OSM — district polygon from OSM relation 8389079 (Nominatim), building ways and multipolygon relations from the Overpass API.
  2. Detection layer — Microsoft Global Buildings (2026-02-03 release) for the two Panama quadkeys. Recall against already-mapped OSM buildings: 86.2%, median shape IoU 0.512.
  3. Difference — UTM 17N, each footprint minus the union of OSM (and, for the second source, Microsoft) polygons buffered by 1.5 m; uncovered parts ≥ 30 m² and ≥ 30% of the footprint become candidates (model-only blobs need ≥ 60 m²). This keeps half-mapped rows of houses instead of dropping them.
  4. Scene check — a DINOv3 ViT-S/16 + UperNet building segmenter (hotosm/dinov3s-buildings, ONNX) is run over all 4,576 z18 tiles of the district; blobs that neither dataset covers are added as source: model candidates, and the same masks score every candidate (cv_cover = share of the footprint within 1.8 m of a predicted building).
  5. Ranking — candidates binned into 400 m cells and ordered by count.

62,709 candidates come from the Microsoft layer (57,106) and 5,603 from the segmentation model alone (parts of a built-up block that neither OSM nor Microsoft covers).

Confidence

verified cv_cover ≥ 0.60 — a building is visible at that location.   probable ≥ 0.30.   unconfirmed < 0.30 — sheds, walls, patios, or the roof is hidden by trees. Calibration: 91 % of already-mapped OSM buildings score verified with the same metric.

Do not bulk-import

Microsoft's footprints are licensed CDLA-Permissive-2.0, not ODbL, and contain false positives. Use this map as a reference: open the same location in iD and trace the building from the Esri imagery. That is also why verified means "a building is here", not "this polygon is the building".

Caveats

  • The segmenter is trained on HOT/OpenAerialMap very-high-resolution tiles; at 0.59 m/px it merges row-houses, so coverage is a screen, not a survey.
  • Footprints mapped with a 1–2 m offset are treated as mapped (buffer), by design.
  • Imagery is from August 2025; anything built later is absent.
  • Small structures below 35 m² uncovered area are not reported.
